MEAN Stack Project

CivicPulse

Empowering Communities Through Civic Engagement

A modern civic engagement platform connecting citizens with municipal authorities to report, track, and resolve civic issues efficiently.

01 / 09

The Problem & Our Solution

⚠️

The Problem

  • ❌ Inefficient complaint registration processes
  • ❌ No transparency in issue resolution
  • ❌ Delayed response from municipal authorities
  • ❌ Lack of citizen participation
  • ❌ No accountability for workers
✨

Our Solution

  • ✅ One-click issue reporting with photos & GPS
  • ✅ Real-time status tracking & notifications
  • ✅ Smart worker assignment system
  • ✅ Gamification with points & badges
  • ✅ Complete analytics dashboard

Real-World Example

🏙️ Scenario: Broken Streetlight in Hyderabad

1

👤 Citizen Spots Issue

Ravi notices a broken streetlight on MG Road. Opens CivicPulse app, takes a photo, and GPS auto-captures location.

2

👨‍💼 Admin Reviews

Municipal admin sees it in dashboard, marks priority as "High", assigns to Electrician worker Suresh in that zone.

3

👷 Worker Resolves

Suresh gets notification, visits location, repairs the light, uploads "before & after" photos as proof.

4

🔔 Citizen Notified

Ravi receives instant notification: "Your complaint has been resolved!" He can rate the service ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

5

🏆 Rewards Earned

Ravi earns +50 points! After 5 successful reports, he receives the "Active Citizen" badge 🎖️
