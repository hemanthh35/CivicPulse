MEAN Stack Project

CivicPulse

Empowering Communities Through Civic Engagement

A modern civic engagement platform connecting citizens with municipal authorities to report, track, and resolve civic issues efficiently.

01 / 06

The Problem & Our Solution

⚠️

The Problem

  • ❌ Inefficient complaint registration processes
  • ❌ No transparency in issue resolution
  • ❌ Delayed response from municipal authorities
  • ❌ Lack of citizen participation
  • ❌ No accountability for workers
✨

Our Solution

  • ✅ One-click issue reporting with photos & GPS
  • ✅ Real-time status tracking & notifications
  • ✅ Smart worker assignment system
  • ✅ Gamification with points & badges
  • ✅ Complete analytics dashboard
02 / 06

Key Features

📱

Easy Reporting

Submit complaints with photos, descriptions, and precise GPS location in seconds.

🗺️

Interactive Maps

Visualize all complaints on a map with Leaflet integration for geographic insights.

🔔

Real-time Notifications

Get instant updates when your complaint status changes or is resolved.

🏆

Gamification

Earn points and badges for active participation. Compete on the leaderboard!

📊

Analytics Dashboard

Comprehensive charts and metrics for admins to track performance.

🔐

Secure Auth

JWT authentication with optional 2FA OTP verification for enhanced security.
