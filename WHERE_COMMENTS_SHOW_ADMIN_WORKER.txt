WHAT YOU CAN DO IN COMMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ADMIN Can:
✓ View all comments from citizens and workers
✓ Post official admin responses
✓ Track conversation history
✓ See timestamps for audit trail
✓ Identify who commented and their role

WORKER Can:
✓ View citizen feedback and requests
✓ Respond to citizen concerns
✓ Coordinate with other workers (if any)
✓ Keep citizen informed about progress
✓ Document their work notes

CITIZEN Can:
(In their own "My Complaints" view - not shown here)
✓ See worker responses
✓ Ask follow-up questions
✓ Share additional information
✓ Provide feedback after resolution


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
COMMENT INFORMATION SHOWN
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For each comment you see:
📝 Text: The actual comment content
👤 Name: Who posted it
🏷️  Role Badge: [citizen] [worker] [admin] - shows user type
📅 Date: When it was posted (Oct 14, Oct 19, etc.)
🕐 Time: Short date format (Oct 14, 2025)


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOW TO ADD A COMMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Open the Complaint Details (click Details button)
2. Scroll to bottom - find "💬 COMMENTS & DISCUSSION" section
3. Look for text box: "Type your comment here..."
4. Click in the text area
5. Type your comment (any length)
6. Click [Post Comment] button
7. Comment appears immediately in the list above!
